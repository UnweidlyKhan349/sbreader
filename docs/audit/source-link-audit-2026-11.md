# Source-link audit (November 14, 2026)

Every set's source link (the set's own folder or document, and every packet link its questions carry) was fetched: Drive folders were walked with their subfolders, and each file's text was split into questions at its ANSWER lines (or at its TOSS-UP/BONUS headings when it has none). Each packet question was looked up in the whole bank by its wording; one that wasn't found was then looked up by its round and slot (TOSS-UP/BONUS and number) and by its rarest words (two of its three rarest in one question of its own set). What none of the three checks found is counted below.

- Files read: 2883 from 101 sets; packet questions read: 122,046 (a file linked from two sets counts twice).
- Not found in their own set by any check: 491 (Mira Loma Question Database 305, every other set 186).
- Of the non-Mira-Loma ones, by what they look like: other 105, geography 34, visual/needs figure or draft note 24, dated / minor history 14, explanation key 6, joke / template 3 (the "other" count includes NSB Regionals 2002 Set B's documented leave-outs). Every kind but "other" is something the inclusion policy leaves out; the 100 "other" are listed at the end.

## Sets that could not be checked from their links

- **ScibowlDB only** (one page per question, no packet file): 2016 Question Exchange, NSB Nationals 2005, ScibowlDB HW.
- **isobowl.com** (the page is an app with no packet text to read): ICSBT 3, NCB Fall 2026, NSBA 4, Trio Online Math Bowl 2026.
- **speriphery.com/query** (a search page, not a packet): LADWP 2023, Lexington Biology Bowl 2026, MOOSE 2021, Yale 2026.
- **LOST 2**: its packets are page scans with no text layer (the bank's copy was made by OCR and checked against the page images).
- **Stuyvesant Question Bank**: its link is a repository page, not a file.
- Also unread: 4 OSTI middle-school PDFs that OSTI's firewall refused (Set 9 RegionalMS_12A, Set 10 17A, Set 12 MSRound-10, Set 13 Round-3A), 5 Google Slides visual-bonus decks and 2 Drive files that export no text.

## What the gaps turned out to be

- **BUMS 2026** is the same event as **Bay Ultimate MS Scibowl**, ingested twice (its Drive folder holds DE1–DE8 and RR1–RR5, the same packets). Every regular question in the BUMS files is in the bank under Bay Ultimate MS Scibowl; the BUMS 2026 set only keeps 30 leftovers, and all 30 are copies of Bay Ultimate questions (26 identical, 4 with a word or two changed). That is why its RR 2 shows two questions and RR 1 and RR 3 don't appear at all. The only BUMS questions in neither set are the "BUMS Special" joke rounds (policy 8). **Suggested fix**: retire the BUMS 2026 set and alias its 30 questions to their Bay Ultimate copies, as the duplicate policy (4) already says.
- **CSBL Rounds 7–10** read as entirely missing only because those PDFs' text layer drops the letters t, r, s, u, v and w; the bank has all four rounds.
- **Science Bowl Subject Sets 1991–93** and the six DOE subject files in the **CSUN** folder: the documented leave-outs (dated facts, place names, prize and spaceflight trivia, wrong keys) plus PDF damage.
- **NOSB**: mostly Marine Policy, Social Sciences and geography questions (policies 6 and 7).
- **SMH 2025** boss battle (policy 8), visual bonuses whose figure was never published (SBST, SBST Spring, Texas 2026 and others; policy 1), and the NSB sets' place-name and "largest producer" questions (policies 2 and 6).
- **Mira Loma Question Database** (305 not found, of about 15,300 in its GitHub files): its files include drafts, unparsed copies and e-mail archives, and the November school-team passes removed 475 questions across Mira Loma, Stuyvesant, Tycho and Evan Zhong (wrong keys, no right choice, missing figures, dated facts). The rest weren't gone through one by one here.
