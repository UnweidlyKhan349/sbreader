A text-based Science Bowl packet reader and database created by Henry Lu.

Its design and features follow QB Reader, the quizbowl reader.

Packets are collected from publicly posted practice packets on scibowl.live, tournament packets from scibowl.stanford.edu and oly.mehvix.com, the official NSB regional sample sets from science.osti.gov, the Science Bowl Periphery packet archive and database, released ISOBowl Premier packets, and a personal archive. If you notice an issue with a question or its subject, please open an issue.

Feel free to contribute to the source code on GitHub (MIT License). Changes are listed in the changelog.

Why SB Reader?

  • Real toss-ups and bonuses from regional, national and invitational packets, and from practice sets written by school teams.
  • Can filter questions by subject, toss-up/bonus, format, level, tournament and round.
  • Play on your own, or with friends in a multiplayer room.
  • Excellent mobile support.
  • Over 100,000 questions from over 100 sets.

How do I play?

  • Play: choose a mode and subjects in the settings on the right, then press Next. The question appears word by word; press Space to buzz, type your answer and press Enter. Toss-ups give you 4 seconds to buzz after the question is read, bonuses 20, and you then have 10 seconds to answer.
  • Multiplayer: type a room name and press Go, or pick a room from the list, and share the room's URL with friends. The first player to buzz locks everyone else out until they've answered.
  • Database: search every question by text, answer or tournament. The set list shows every tournament, its packets and their questions.
  • Star a question in the Database or the question history, then choose Starred Questions in Play to practice just those.

What does making an account do?

Making an account gives you the following features:

  • Keeps your starred questions on every device you log in on.
  • Lets you track your toss-up and bonus stats.
  • Keeps your settings (theme, reading speed, multiplayer name).

Without an account, everything works and is saved in your browser.

Does it cost money to create an account?

No. SB Reader is completely free.

How are answers graded?

Automatically, following Science Bowl conventions. If the grader gets one wrong, use I was wrong / I was right (or q) to flip it.

  • Multiple choice: give the letter (W, X, Y or Z) or the exact text of the choice.
  • Short answer: capitalisation, punctuation and spacing don't matter, and the packet's "accept" and "do not accept" notes are followed.
  • Numbers are compared whole, with their sign. Scientific notation can be typed as 3.2 x 10^23, 3.2*10^23 or 3.2e23; a decimal matches a fraction in lowest terms with the same value (0.5 = 1/2); units may be written out or abbreviated (10 joules = 10 J).
  • "Identify all" questions: give the item numbers in any form (1 and 3, 13, 1, 3). Ranking questions must be in the right order.
  • Multi-part answers: every part must be given, separated by commas, semicolons or "and".

What are the keyboard shortcuts?

  • Space: buzz, Enter: submit (in Multiplayer, also open the chat)
  • n or s: next question / skip, p: pause / resume
  • k: show / hide the last question, t: star / unstar it
  • q: I was wrong / I was right, e: show / hide settings

Shortcuts are ignored while you're typing in a text box (except Enter).
