Multiplayer

Jump into a public lobby, create a room and share the code (click it in the room to copy an invite link), or join a friend's room. Everyone can buzz — first in locks the rest out until they've answered. Rooms don't depend on whoever started them: anyone can leave and the game carries on.

Public lobbies

Open rooms anyone can join: one mixing every subject, and one for each subject except energy. A lobby’s subject is fixed; everything else can be changed from the room settings.
