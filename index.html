Free Science Bowl practice questions

SB Reader is a free practice site for the National Science Bowl and other Science Bowl–style tournaments, for high school and middle school teams. Every question comes from a real tournament packet: regional and nationals sample sets, invitationals such as MIT, Stanford and Yale, and community question sets. Each one keeps its tossup or bonus type, multiple-choice or short-answer format, round and source packet, and visual bonuses are shown with their figures.

Questions cover all six Science Bowl subjects: biology, chemistry, physics, math, earth & space science and energy. Search the question catalog, drill on your own in solo practice with tossup and bonus timers and automatic grading, or open a multiplayer room and buzz against teammates online. There's nothing to install and no account to make; bookmarks are saved in your browser.

Questions sourced from publicly posted practice packets on scibowl.live, tournament packets from scibowl.stanford.edu, oly.mehvix.com, the official NSB regional sample sets from science.osti.gov, the Science Bowl Periphery packet archive and database, released ISOBowl Premier packets, and a personal archive. Answers, subjects, and rounds were parsed automatically and may occasionally contain errors — use the catalog to spot-check anything that looks off.
