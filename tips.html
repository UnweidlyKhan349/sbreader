Tips & Instructions

New to SB Reader? Start here for quick guidance on playing, searching, and getting the most out of the site.

Getting Started
  • Use Play for practice on your own, toss-ups and bonuses alike.
  • Join Multiplayer to play with friends or in open rooms.
  • Use the theme button at the top right to switch between light, night and automatic.
Searching the Database
  • Open Search to find questions by text, answer, or tournament, searching all text, the question only, or the answer only.
  • Use Set List to browse available packets.
  • Explore Frequency List for answerline trends.
Accounts & Progress
  • Create an account to keep your starred questions on every device and track your tossup stats, bonus stats and graphs.
  • Without one, everything still works and is saved in your browser.
  • Visit Account for your stats, graphs and starred questions, and to change your username, email and password.
Help & Community
  • Read more on the About page, including how answers are graded and the keyboard shortcuts.
  • Use a question's Report link if something about it is wrong.
  • Report issues or request features on GitHub.
Multiplayer
  • Join Multiplayer rooms or create your own by typing a room name.
  • Rooms you create are public by default; you can make them private when you create them, or in the sidebar.
  • Customize question settings (subjects, tournaments, years, rounds) before starting.
  • Share the room link with friends to invite them directly (click the room's name to copy it).
  • Use the chat feature (enter) to communicate with other players in the room.
  • Choose between Random Questions and Select by Set Name.
