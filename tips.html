Tips & Instructions

New to SB Reader? Start here for how to practice, answer, search and keep track of your progress.

Getting Started

  • Open Play to practice on your own, or Multiplayer to play with friends or in an open room.
  • Press Next (or n) to start. The question appears word by word, as a moderator would read it.
  • Use the toggle at the top right of the page to switch between light, night and automatic themes.

Playing

  • Pick what to practice in the settings: Random Questions from the subjects, tournaments, years and rounds you choose, Select by Set Name to play one tournament's packets in order, or Starred Questions.
  • Buzz with space as soon as you know the answer. A toss-up gives you 4 seconds to buzz after it has been read, a bonus 20, and you then have 10 seconds to answer.
  • Turn off Type to answer to grade yourself: after you buzz, press Reveal (or space) to see the answer. It counts as right; press I was wrong (or q) if it wasn't.
  • Turn on Allow rebuzzes to keep trying after a wrong answer, or turn off Enable timer to take your time.
  • Slow the reading speed down while you're learning, and speed it up as you get faster.
  • Your stats line shows correct and incorrect answers, points and celerity (how early you buzz correctly). Clear stats starts them again from zero.
  • Clear all filters puts every subject, tournament and round back.

Answering

  • Multiple choice: type the letter (W, X, Y or Z) or the exact text of the choice.
  • Short answer: capitals, punctuation and spacing don't matter, and the packet's accept and do-not-accept notes are followed.
  • Numbers: give the whole number with its sign. Scientific notation can be typed as 3.2 x 10^23, 3.2*10^23 or 3.2e23, and units can be written out or abbreviated (10 joules or 10 J).
  • Math: type expressions with ^ for powers, sqrt or √ for roots and brackets wherever they matter: (1 + √5)/2 isn't 1 + √5/2.
  • "Identify all" questions: give the item numbers in any form (1 and 3, 13, 1, 3). Ranking questions need the right order.
  • Multi-part answers: give every part, separated by commas, semicolons or "and".
  • If the grader gets your answer wrong, press I was right (or q) to override it, and Report the question if its answer is wrong.

Searching the Database

  • Open Search to find questions. Every word you type must appear in the question, its answer or its tournament name; with Ignore word order off, the words must appear together as typed. Capital letters never matter.
  • Each question's Stats show how everyone has done on it: how often it was heard, answered right or wrong or with an interrupt, the average celerity and the points it gave.
  • Browse every tournament and its packets in the Set List.
  • See which answers come up most often in each subject in the Frequency List.
  • Star a question (t on the last question) to practice it later with Starred Questions.

Multiplayer

  • Join one of the permanent rooms, pick a public room from the list, or type a room name to create your own.
  • Rooms are public by default; tick Make room private to keep yours off the list. A private room's creator owns it: they can mute and ban players, and make the room controlled, so only they can change its settings.
  • Click the room's name to copy its link, and send it to friends to invite them.
  • The first player to buzz locks everyone else out until they've answered. Press enter to chat with the room.
  • Anyone can change the room's settings (unless it's controlled), including the switches for rebuzzes, skips and the timer.

Accounts & Progress

  • Everything works without an account, saved in your browser.
  • A free account keeps your starred questions and settings on every device you log in on, and builds a stats page of your accuracy by subject and tournament.
  • Manage your username and password on the Account page.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • space: buzz; enter: submit (in Multiplayer, also open the chat)
  • n or s: next question / skip; p: pause / resume
  • k: show / hide the last question; t: star / unstar it
  • q: I was wrong / I was right; e: show / hide settings

Shortcuts are ignored while you're typing in a text box (except enter).
